Questions couples ask

How do I work out how many days we've been together?

Count the full days from the day you got together to today. The counter above does it for you: the day you got together is day 0, and every midnight adds one.

How many days is 6 months together?

About 182 days. The exact number depends on which months you went through, from 181 to 184 days. Pick your date above for your exact count.

When is our 1,000th day?

About 2 years and 9 months after you got together. The counter shows the exact date of your next round-number day, like day 500 or day 1,000.

What do couples do for 100 days together?

Day 100 is a big one for many couples, especially in Korea, where it is a celebrated milestone. Couples often swap a small gift, write a letter or recreate their first date. Pick your date above to see when your day 100 is.

What is a monthsary?

Your monthly anniversary: the same date each month as the day you got together. If you got together on 14 February, every 14th is your monthsary.
