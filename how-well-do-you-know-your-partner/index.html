Questions couples ask

How well do you know your partner quiz: how does it work?

Answer 10 questions about yourself, then send the link to your partner. They guess your answers one by one and get a score out of 10. Then they can make their own quiz for you.

Is the partner quiz free?

Yes. No sign-up, no app needed. Your answers are stored only in the link you share, not on our servers.

What questions should I ask to see if my partner knows me?

Everyday ones work best: comfort food, pet peeves, how you handle stress, your dream trip and what makes you feel loved. They are easy to answer but show who has been paying attention.

What if my partner gets a low score?

Treat it as a starting point, not a test they failed. Go through the answers together. Most couples learn something new, and it makes a good conversation for date night.

Can we do it for long distance?

Yes. Send the link by text or DM. It works on any phone or computer, wherever your partner is.
