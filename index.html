FAQ

Good questions.

Do we both need the app?
Yes. LoveCo is made for two phones. Download it, then send your partner your invite link. They tap it, get the app, and you're paired.
Do we both need to pay for Plus?
No. One subscription covers both of you. When either of you has Plus, the other gets it automatically.
Is it only for long distance couples?
Not at all. It's for any couple: across the world, across town, or across the sofa. Doodles and games are just as fun when you're in the same room.
Who can see what we share?
Only the two of you. Answers stay sealed until you've both replied, and we never sell your data. Read our Privacy Policy.
Is there an Android version?
Not yet. LoveCo is on iPhone for now.
What if we break up or I want to leave?
You can unpair or delete your account any time from Settings. Deleting your account removes your data.
