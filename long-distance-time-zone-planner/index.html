Questions couples ask

What is the best time to call in a long-distance relationship?

When you're both awake and free. Put in both time zones and your usual wake-up and bedtimes above and the planner shows the hours that overlap, in both of your times.

How do long-distance couples handle a big time difference?

Most pick one fixed daily call in the overlap, for example your morning and their evening, and fill the rest with messages they can read later. A goodnight message timed for their bedtime goes a long way.

What if our awake hours don't overlap at all?

Then one of you shifts a little: an early alarm twice a week, or a later night at the weekend. The planner shows how many hours you would need to move.

Does the planner handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your device's time zone data, so the times and the difference are right for today, including when one country has changed its clocks and the other hasn't yet.

Is anything I enter saved?

No. The planner runs in your browser and saves nothing. The link in the address bar only holds the two time zones so you can bookmark or share them.
