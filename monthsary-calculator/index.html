The quick answer

A monthsary is your monthly anniversary: the same date every month as the day you got together. Got together on 14 February? Every 14th is your monthsary. If you got together on the 29th, 30th or 31st, the monthsary falls on the last day of shorter months.

Questions couples ask

What is a monthsary?

Your monthly anniversary: the same date each month as the day you got together. The word is especially common in the Philippines, where many couples mark every monthsary in their first year.

How do I calculate our monthsary?

Take the day of the month you got together. That date every month is your monthsary, and the number is how many months have passed. The calculator above does the counting and shows the next date.

What if we got together on the 31st?

In months with fewer days, your monthsary falls on the last day of the month, for example 30 April or 28 February.

Is the 12th monthsary an anniversary?

Yes. Most couples call month 12 their first anniversary, and month 24 their second.

What do couples do on a monthsary?

Small things: a sweet message, a little gift, their favourite food or a photo from the month. The point is noticing the date, not spending money.
